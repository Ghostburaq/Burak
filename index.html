The Story

I started late.
I didn't start small.

I came to sports the long way around. Volleyball in high school, then nothing that stuck — until I picked up a barbell at 24. That was it. That was the thing.

The years after weren't clean. There were injuries, setbacks, and comebacks that took longer than I wanted. I took up kickboxing. I ran a demolition derby car. I raised my kid on my own and kept training through all of it.

Then I stepped on a bodybuilding stage for the first time and walked off with two first-place finishes and a second. Today I'm an IFBB Pro, a certified fitness trainer, and co-owner of the meal prep company MomBodz.

What I care about now is simple: helping women build real strength and the kind of confidence that doesn't wobble when life gets loud.
