The Story

I started late.
Then I went pro.

I came to sports late. Volleyball in high school was the start. Then at 24 I found weightlifting — and that changed everything.

The road was not clean. Highs, lows, and the injuries that come with pushing your limits. I kept going. I picked up kickboxing. I ran a demolition derby car. I raised my kid on my own and handled more than I can count.

Then I stepped on a bodybuilding stage for the first time and walked off with two first-place wins and a second. Today I'm an IFBB Pro and a certified trainer.

My work is simple: help women build real strength and unshakable confidence — and become the best version of themselves.
